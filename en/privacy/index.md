# Privacy Policy

Apps covered: Trace and Learn: Hiragana & Katakana, and なぞっておぼえる かんじ1年〜6年 (Kanji, Grades 1–6) ("the Apps").

## 1. Information we collect

The Apps collect no information about you. They never ask for a name, e-mail address, location, photos or contacts, and they do not use any device identifier.

## 2. Network

The Apps do not connect to the internet. They contain no advertising, no analytics, and no code that sends anything to an external service.

## 3. Information stored on your device

Practice records (which letters have been traced) and settings (such as the chosen voice and language) are stored on that device only. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting an App also deletes its records.

## 4. Access to device features

The Apps do not request access to the camera, microphone, location, photos or contacts.

## 5. Sharing with third parties

Because no information is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties.

## 6. Use by children

The Apps are made for children. As stated above, they collect no information and do not connect to the internet.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the change will be posted on this page.

## 8. Information handled when you contact us

Outside the Apps, on this website, there are two ways to reach us. **Neither is opened from inside an App** — the Apps contain no external links.

- **E-mail** [chemise_merry0o@icloud.com](mailto:chemise_merry0o@icloud.com) — your address and message are used only to reply to you and to improve the Apps, and are not given to third parties
- **Contact form (GitHub)** [https://github.com/nazotte/nazotte.github.io/issues/new/choose](https://github.com/nazotte/nazotte.github.io/issues/new/choose) — a service of GitHub, Inc. **What you write and your GitHub account name become public.**
  GitHub's own privacy policy applies there. For anything private, please use e-mail
